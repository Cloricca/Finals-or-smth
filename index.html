    How to Clear Your Backlog

    Follow these ordered steps using mixed list styles (Roman numerals, alphabetical sub-steps, and square/circle sub-bullets):

    1. Pick one focus game from the catalog
      1. Filter by your favorite genre first.
      2. Save 2–3 backups to My Backlog.
    2. Check your pace in the Playtime Planner
      • Aim for a realistic daily goal (1–2 hours).
      • Use Main Story Only for faster clears.
    3. Share feedback if a playtime estimate looks off.

    What Makes a Great Backlog Pick

    • Clear main-story pacing under 35 hours
    • Distinct visual identity:
      • Hand-crafted pixel or vector art
      • Readable HUD and instant save states
    • Fair difficulty options for weeknight play
